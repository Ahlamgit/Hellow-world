# Areeba — Risk Assessment

**Status:** Pre-implementation  
**Date:** 2026-07-23  
**Scope:** Phases A–E (payment attempt model, Areeba adapter, webhooks, booking integration, client checkout)

## Risk register

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|----|------|------------|--------|------------|
| R1 | Enum renumbering corrupts payment statuses | Low (if followed) | Critical | Append ints 7–8 only; never change 1–6 |
| R2 | Dual completion (client confirm + webhook) double-advances booking | Medium | High | Idempotent finalize; Completed short-circuit; unique slot reservation |
| R3 | Delayed webhook for abandoned attempt completes wrong attempt | Medium | High | Correlate by gateway transaction/session; only one Completed; validate booking still `AwaitingPayment` |
| R4 | Mobile still auto-confirms | High if missed | High | Phase E removes pattern; production Verify fails until paid |
| R5 | Areeba signature scheme differs from assumed HMAC | Medium | High | Confirm with boarding; fail-closed; feature-test in sandbox (Phase 2) |
| R6 | Silent Development fallback in production | Medium | Critical | Areeba adapter must not fall back; readiness gate |
| R7 | Unique `TransactionReference` fails on historical duplicates | Low–Med | Medium | Pre-migration duplicate query; null or dedupe before index |
| R8 | Moyasar regressions during coexistence | Medium | Medium | Keep Moyasar path; regression tests; provider switch only |
| R9 | Amount/currency mismatch accepted | Low | Critical | VerifyAsync mandatory before Completed |
| R10 | Secrets leaked in logs | Low | High | Redaction rules; never log passwords/PAN |
| R11 | Phase 1C / RN distraction | Medium | Medium | Explicitly blocked until payment stable |
| R12 | Filtered indexes mis-generated by EF | Medium | Medium | Hand-review migration SQL (Phase 1A lesson) |

## Residual risk after mitigations

Acceptable for staging rollout; production cutover only after sandbox verification and monitoring plan.
