## 3. Clear space and minimum size

| Rule | Requirement |
|------|-------------|
| Clear space | Minimum padding equal to height of the home/tool symbol in the mark |
| Minimum digital size | 24×24px for favicon contexts; 48×48px minimum for in-app mark |
| App icon safe zone | Follow iOS/Android platform safe-area guidelines |

---

## 4. Background compatibility

| Background | Rule |
|------------|------|
| Light | Use primary full-color logo |
| Dark | Use light-compatible variant (refined deliverable) |
| Photography | Place on solid overlay or approved contrast panel |
| Orange brand fields | Use mark-only or approved monochrome variant if contrast requires |

---

## 5. Approved usage

- Customer mobile app icon and splash
- Provider mobile app icon and splash (same brand system)
- Administrator portal header and login
- Marketing materials approved by Project Owner / Business Owner
- Store listing assets

---

## 6. Prohibited modifications

| Prohibition | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| **Redesign** | Do not alter symbol concept, home/tool metaphor, or brand meaning |
| **Color change** | Do not change approved orange identity (except approved light/dark variants) |
| **Distortion** | No stretch, skew, or non-uniform scale |
| **Effects** | No drop shadows, glows, gradients, or outlines not in approved assets |
| **Rotation** | Do not rotate the mark |
| **Partial crop** | Do not crop symbol elements |
| **Unapproved lockups** | Do not combine with third-party logos without governance approval |
| **Low resolution** | Do not use pixelated or compressed artifacts |
| **Substitution** | Do not replace with alternate icons or wordmarks |

---

## 7. Arabic identity preservation

- Arabic wordmark **خدماتي** must remain legible and culturally appropriate
- RTL layouts: logo placement must respect reading direction
- Do not transliterate Arabic brand into unofficial variants
