## 5. Prohibited modifications

| Prohibition | Detail |
|-------------|--------|
| Redesign | Do not create a new symbol or alter brand meaning |
| Replacement | Do not substitute a different logo |
| Color drift | Do not change approved orange identity |
| Distortion | No stretch, skew, or non-proportional scale |
